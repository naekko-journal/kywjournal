Karmic Story: The Nobleman Who Was Cursed by a Gardener.
posted by kyeonwoo  ·  14 Oct 1990  ·  [personal]

- - - - - - - - - -

The gardener waited, and waited.

Each day passed, one flower was planted.
Each month passed, one flower grew.
Each year passed, one flower withered.

Until her lungs filled with pollen and petals.
She suffocated and died.

And even then, the one who made the promise
Never kept it.

- - - - - - - - - -

My name is Kim Yeonwoo.

I did not write this as fiction.

The blood has roots. The roots have memory. And the memory does not forget what the heart refuses to say. I am writing this because I am running out of time to hide anything at all.

If you found this — if your search brought you here the way mine once brought me to others who came before — then you already know what I know.

You are not the first.

And I am sorry that you are not the last.
